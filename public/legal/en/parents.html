Parents' Information

PRIME TIME: SHOWDOWN · Effective date: 20 October 2026 · version 2

1. The Game

PRIME TIME: SHOWDOWN is a browser game featuring stylised combat and virtual game rewards. The developer does not report realistic blood or graphic injuries, but an official age classification has not been established here. A game being suitable or appealing to a child does not automatically mean the child may independently register a PRIME ID account.

2. Guest Play

Guest preferences and progress are stored on the device and are not sent to the PRIME NETWORK server. Guest play is not entirely anonymous: when the site is visited and the Game checks its connection to the server, technical connection data such as the IP address and browser data is processed. See the Privacy Policy.

3. PRIME ID Accounts

PRIME ID provides cloud game progress and account access across devices. It requires an email address; passwords are protected using server-side hashing. A minor's ability to register independently depends on applicable law and the controls actually implemented: the Game asks for the age at registration, and PRIME ID is not available under 13. Where verified parental permission is legally necessary, registration must wait until an adequate process exists.

4. Privacy and Safety

According to the current project description, the Game does not yet have player-to-player chat, paid purchases, third-party advertising or behavioural analytics; the operator will update these notices when the features change. Children should avoid putting real names, addresses, schools, phone numbers and other personal details into nicknames, and should not share passwords.

5. Parents' Requests

Parents or legal guardians can contact primetimeshowdown.official@gmail.com about information relating to a child, account correction or deletion and the legal requirements for guardian verification. We may use a proportionate and secure verification process; please do not email account passwords.

6. Other Policies

See the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and, where genuinely applicable, a separate Personal Data Consent form. Checking a box cannot replace legally required parental authorisation.
