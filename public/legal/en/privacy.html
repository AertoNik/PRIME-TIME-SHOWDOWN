Email: primetimeshowdown.official@gmail.com

1. Scope

1.1. This Policy describes the processing of personal information when visitors access https://primetimeshowdown.online, play as guests, register for PRIME ID, use cloud saves, receive service emails or contact support.

1.2. We seek to collect only information needed for specified service, security or legal purposes. We do not sell players' personal information.

1.3. According to the current technical audit, the current build has no real-money payments, third-party advertising network, external behavioural analytics or player-to-player chat. This description will be updated before such features are introduced.

2. Information We Process

Account data: nickname, email address, account identifier, a protected password hash rather than the raw password, email verification status, authentication and session records.

Game data: settings, achievements, levels, rewards and in-game currency, character state, progression, current cloud saves and historical save records to the extent actually used by the service.

Security and technical data: IP addresses or derived technical identifiers where actually processed, request timestamps, relevant browser or User-Agent details, login and security events, anti-abuse challenges, rate limits and errors. This information is used to operate and secure the service.

Legal acceptance records: account reference, document identifier and version, language, acceptance timestamp and method; an age group (“13–17” or “18+”, without the exact age or a date of birth) and the country — only if it was asked — for the account eligibility check of the deployed build.

Support data: sender email, correspondence and attachments a user chooses to send to our support Gmail address. Please do not email us passwords or identity documents without a separate secure procedure.

Guest-play data: local game progress and preferences are stored in the browser on the device and are not sent to the PRIME NETWORK server. When the site is opened, connection data is processed by the GitHub Pages hosting; when the Game checks its connection to the PRIME NETWORK server, the server's request log records the IP address, browser data and time of the request (see section 5). Guest play is therefore not entirely anonymous.

Ordinary PRIME ID signup does not require a player's real name, passport, phone number or precise location.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Creating and operating PRIME ID, signing users in, verifying addresses, recovering accounts and keeping requested cloud progress: contract performance where that basis is legally available, or another appropriate applicable basis.
  • Preventing fraud, spam, unauthorized access and inconsistent game saves: security and legitimate interests or other applicable grounds assessed against players' rights and age.
  • Sending necessary service emails: completing user-requested functions and protecting accounts.
  • Handling inquiries and legal obligations: applicable contractual, legal or other legitimate bases.
  • Operations requiring consent: specific, separate, valid consent where required; consent is not automatically the basis for all necessary account processing.

4. Service Providers and International Transfers

Timeweb Cloud / PocketBase hosts account and cloud-save data. The principal PocketBase VPS and account database are hosted in the Netherlands (NL-15). Backups are created on the server and may also be kept in access-controlled storage by the operator.

Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) sends verification and password-reset emails. The data sent can include the recipient address, sender, subject, message content and delivery metadata. Resend states that it stores email content and logs in the United States, even when an EU sending region is selected. For its standard Free/Pro/Scale plans, the stated email and log retention period is 30 days, subject to Resend's published service conditions.

GitHub Pages serves static game files and may process connection metadata to deliver the site. It does not hold the main PRIME ID account database.

Google Gmail receives user inquiries at primetimeshowdown.official@gmail.com. Google may process submitted message contents and metadata under applicable service terms. Correspondence is retained while necessary to respond to requests, meet legal requirements or protect legitimate interests.

DuckDNS provides the DNS name of the API server. Game requests and account data go directly to the server in the Netherlands and do not pass through DuckDNS.

Cross-border processing, where applicable, is subject to legal assessment, provider safeguards and required notices or filings.

5. Retention

  • Active PRIME ID and game saves: while the account remains active and processing remains needed for the service, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • Newly registered accounts whose email is never verified: deleted automatically seven days after registration; accounts created before this rule was introduced are not affected.
  • PocketBase request logs: seven days; they are not included in backups.
  • Deleted-account identifiers kept only to prevent restoration from old backups: 60 days in a separate deletion ledger (without email or nickname). Even pseudonymous identifiers are protected against inappropriate access.
  • Resend email data and logs: 30 days on standard Free/Pro/Scale plans; Resend backups: seven days, subject to provider conditions.
  • PocketBase backups: maintained on the Netherlands server and may additionally be stored in access-controlled copies kept by the operator as necessary for incident recovery. Historical backups may temporarily include data already deleted from the live database; a technical deletion ledger prevents deleted accounts from returning after a restore.
  • Gmail correspondence: kept while needed to process requests or to comply with applicable legal requirements; correspondence may then be deleted.
  • Other technical counters and challenge records: retained for the limited period necessary for service security and request-rate restrictions; expired records are periodically cleaned up.

6. Security

We apply appropriate measures within the service architecture, including access restrictions, HTTPS, protected credentials, server authorization and anti-abuse controls. No service can promise absolute availability or security.

7. Children

The Game may appeal to children under 13. The rules for collecting children's information depend on applicable law, the audience, marketing, actual guest-mode network activity, PRIME ID functions and any legally required parental process. A statement in the Terms is not treated as a substitute for required age assurance or verifiable parental consent.

At PRIME ID registration the Game asks a neutral age question. Registration is not available under 13. For ages 13–17 it is available only where independent registration has been confirmed; otherwise it remains unavailable until a guardian-consent process exists. Guest play is available without registration. See Parents' Information.

8. Your Rights and Contact

Depending on applicable law, users and legal guardians may request access, correction, export, erasure, restriction, objection or withdrawal of applicable consent, and may complain to a competent supervisory authority.

PRIME ID (“My data”) provides a JSON data export and account deletion with password confirmation. Additional requests can be sent to primetimeshowdown.official@gmail.com. We may reasonably verify account ownership or guardian authority; never email us your password. Statutory response times and lawful exceptions apply. Withdrawal does not retroactively invalidate lawful prior processing or stop processing based on a separate legal ground.

9. Browser Storage and Cookies

The Game uses necessary browser storage to maintain login state, sessions, guest progress and preferences (including the language) and — if an age under 13 is given in the age question — a one-day marker of that, without the age itself. Information may remain until the user signs out, clears site data or it is replaced by newer data. Optional tracking would be separately assessed and, if required, consented to before activation.
