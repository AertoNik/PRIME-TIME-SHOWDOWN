On the operator's behalf, YANDEX LLC (16 Lva Tolstogo St., Moscow, 119021) processes the data through its Yandex Metrica service, under the Yandex Metrica Terms of Use.

2. Purpose

To count visits to primetimeshowdown.online and see where visitors come from: search, links or ads. This helps assess ads and improve the site.

Metrica plays no part in PRIME ID sign-in, saves, purchases or the game itself.

3. Data

Sent to Metrica:

  • identifiers in Metrica cookies (for example _ym_uid);
  • IP address;
  • browser, device and operating system details, screen size and language;
  • the page address. Metrica starts only on an address without service parameters: only advertising labels are allowed (utm_*, yclid, ysclid, gclid, fbclid, vkclid, erid and similar). It does not start when you return from the payment page;
  • the referring address (from other sites, browsers usually report only the site address);
  • visit time and duration;
  • the page title.

Never sent:

  • nickname, email address, identifier and other PRIME ID data;
  • game progress and saves;
  • order and payment details;
  • passwords and tokens.

Session replay (Webvisor), which would record page activity and form input, is off. So are click maps and outbound-link tracking.

4. Processing

By automated means:

  • collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation and storage;
  • clarification, retrieval and use;
  • transfer to YANDEX LLC, which processes the data on the operator's behalf;
  • depersonalisation, blocking, deletion and destruction.

5. Duration

Consent stays in force until you withdraw it.

Metrica cookies stay in the browser for the periods the service sets: the main identifier for up to one year.

Aggregated statistics are kept in Yandex Metrica under its terms and deleted together with the site's counter.

6. Giving or withdrawing consent

Without consent, Metrica is not loaded and sets no cookies. Declining does not limit the game.

You can withdraw consent at any time with the button below. Metrica cookies on this site are then deleted and collection stops. If the game is open in another tab at that moment, collection there stops once that tab is reloaded.

To block Metrica on all sites, use the Yandex Metrica Blocker browser extension.

7. Children

If an age under 14 was entered at the PRIME ID age question, Metrica is not switched on for that device.

For a visitor under 14, a parent or legal guardian decides on consent.
